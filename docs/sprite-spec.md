# Sprite spec

All art is generated by `Tools/sprites/build.py` from the modules in `Tools/sprites/characters/`.
Pixel art, one shared palette (`palette.py`), nearest-neighbour filtering, 1 world unit per pixel.

Outputs per animation:
- frames in `App/Resources/Assets.xcassets/Sprites.spriteatlas/<sprite>_<anim>_<n>.imageset`
- `App/Resources/Sprites.json` — size, anchor, frame count, fps and loop flag (read by `SpriteLibrary`)
- `Tools/sprites/out/sheets/<sprite>_<anim>.png`, `out/preview/<sprite>_<anim>.gif` and
  `out/contact/<sprite>.png` for review (not committed)

Sizes are frame sizes; the figure inside is smaller and anchored at its feet (`cx`, `ground`). Enemies face left in the atlas.

## How characters are drawn

Characters are posed, not painted. The hero is an upright chibi cat drawn by `characters/hero.py`'s
`chibi(canvas, HeroPose, cx, ground)`: leg and arm angles, torso lean (90 = flat out), cape angle and ripple,
tail angle and curl, eyes and ears; `running(phase)` gives the run cycle. The ghost cats use the four-legged
`cat(canvas, CatPose, cx, ground, coat)`: leg angles (0° = straight down, positive = forward), body
stretch and tilt, tail angle and curl, eyes and ears; `gallop(phase)` gives the run cycle. Humanoids can
still use `draw.humanoid(canvas, Body, Pose, cx, ground)`. `Canvas.outline()` adds the dark 1px outline
afterwards. The forest critters (`forest_critters.py`), the citadel critters (`citadel_critters.py`; black stone with an ember
rim from `critters.rim`, lava ramping to a white-hot core), the haunted critters (`haunted_critters.py`; spirits are see-through
with a bright outline, magic is magenta) and the frost critters (`frost_critters.py`; slash arcs are glowing ice
crescents laid on after the outline, hits are a white flash then ice sparkles) are built from shaded parts in `critters.py` (`shaded` lights each part's top-left
rim and darkens the far rim, `face` draws the big cat eyes with a shine, `ear`, `sparkle`). Death frames come from `vanish()` (flash, then sink or rise while fading), `shatter()` (breaks
into blocks that fly and fall; also used for crumbling platforms) or `topple()`; `see_through()` lowers
opacity for ghosts and fades.
The four world bosses follow `docs/concept/Pixel Boss Atlas_ Feline Realms.png` (reference only) and share
`Tools/sprites/bosskit.py`: each body part is drawn in one base colour inside `with part(canvas, ramp, edge)`,
which rim-lights its top-left, shades its bottom-right and gives it its own inner outline. It also has `blob`
(furry ellipse), `tube`/`bezier` (tails, necks), `crystal`, `acorn`, `flame`, `flame_jet`, `smoke` and `halo`.
The boss hazards are drawn in `characters/boss_hazards.py` with the same helpers and palette (`SQ_`, `GL_`,
`QN_`, `GM_` colours).
Draw facing right; pass `faces_left=True` for enemies. Set `cx`/`ground` on the `Sprite` so the
game anchors the feet, not the frame corner.
