# Privacy Policy — Simon the Dragon Slayer

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Simon the Dragon Slayer ("the game") is published by Bogdan Pryvalov. This policy explains what
the game does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

## Information the game collects

None. The game:

- has no accounts, sign-in or registration;
- does not connect to the internet and works fully offline;
- contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools;
- includes no third-party software that collects data;
- does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: which levels you have unlocked, your best
scores, your hero's experience, and whether sound is on. This information never leaves your
device and is not sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it.

## Information from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may
give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as download counts and crash reports.
This information comes from Apple, cannot be used to identify you, and is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
