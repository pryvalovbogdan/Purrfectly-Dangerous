# Privacy Policy — Purrfectly Dangerous

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Purrfectly Dangerous ("the game") is published by Bogdan Pryvalov. This policy explains what
the game does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing. Optional purchases
are handled entirely by Apple, and an optional backup goes to your own iCloud account.

## Information the game collects

None. The game:

- has no accounts, sign-in or registration;
- works fully offline; it only goes online through Apple's own services, for the optional
  purchases and the iCloud backup described below, and never to the developer's or anyone else's
  servers;
- contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools;
- includes no third-party software that collects data;
- does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: which levels you have unlocked, your best scores,
your hero's experience, the fish you have collected, the cats you own, and whether sound is on.
This information is not sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it, except
for the iCloud backup below.

## In-app purchases

The game sells optional "golden fish", which can be spent on cats (every cat can also be won by
playing). Purchases are made through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple. The developer
never receives or stores your name, payment card, billing details or Apple Account. The game only
receives, from Apple, a confirmation that a purchase went through and an anonymous transaction
number, which it keeps so that the same purchase is never counted twice. Purchases are governed by
Apple's terms and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## iCloud backup

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps a small backup in your own iCloud account so that
your golden fish and the cats you own survive deleting the game or moving to a new device: the
transaction numbers and golden fish of your purchases, and which cats you own. It is stored by
Apple in your iCloud account, is visible only to this game on your devices, and is never sent to the
developer. You can delete it in your device's iCloud settings. If iCloud is off, nothing is backed
up.

## Information from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may
give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as download counts and crash reports.
This information comes from Apple, cannot be used to identify you, and is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
